Japanese live subtitles

Plays in another tab, English subtitles appear here. Listens to the tab itself, so computer volume, mute and headphones don't matter. Runs on your computer. Your audio never leaves it; only the speech model is downloaded once.

Click Start, choose the tab with your video, and tick "Also share tab audio". Keep the video's own volume up a little and the tab unmuted.
Subtitles will appear here.
